# Copy review: Shiftplate prototype v0.1

> Real run, fictional product. Shiftplate is the made-up product from the [PRD Generator showcase](../../../../../product/skills/prd-generator/examples/shiftplate/). The prototype is [`prototype/index.html`](prototype/index.html), built by a coding agent from the PRD. This review writes the copy for v0.2; the fixed file is [`prototype/index-v0.2-copy.html`](prototype/index-v0.2-copy.html).

**Product:** rebuilds a nurse's week of meals around food already prepped in the fridge when their shifts change. Never a diet app.
**User:** hospital nurses on rotating 12-hour day and night shifts who already meal prep.
**State:** usually just off a 12-hour shift, tired, on a phone, often one-handed. Tone: plain and warm, shortest clear form, no cheerleading.
**Source of truth:** the [PRD](../../../../../product/skills/prd-generator/examples/shiftplate/2-PRD.md). Its definitions (portion, gap meal, slot) are precise for the team, but two of them shouldn't reach the screen.
**Screens:** Home, Fridge (called "Log Prep" in v0.1), Meal plan. Reviewed with the seed data and the default date (Fri 20 Nov).

## Summary

The biggest problem is copy that isn't true: a banner asks whether to regenerate a plan that has already changed, and a dish past its use-by date disappears from the plan without a word, while the fridge count still includes it. There are 20 findings: 3 Blocker, 7 Major, 7 Minor and 3 Polish. Fix the three Blockers first (C1–C3), because two of them touch food safety, then adopt the glossary, which settles most of the rest in one pass.

## Glossary

The prototype uses seven words for food in the fridge and three for the plan. One term each:

| Concept | Use | Don't use | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Something cooked ahead | dish | item, prep (as a noun), data | "Chicken curry" is a dish |
| One serving of a dish | portion | serving, meal, qty, quantity | "1 portion", "3 portions" |
| Where prepped food lives | fridge | inventory, stored, logged | Covers the freezer too until the product separates them |
| Putting a dish in | add | log, submit, enter | "Add food", "Add to fridge" |
| Taking a dish out | remove | delete | Removing food isn't deleting data |
| The days someone works | shifts | rota, D / N, mutation | "Rota" is fine for UK-only use; pick one and keep it |
| A kind of day | day shift, night shift, day off | D, N | Chips can show "Day", "Night", "Off" |
| The week of meals | meal plan | plan generated by the algorithm, output, optimised plan | |
| Changing the plan | update | optimise, regenerate | The plan updates itself when shifts change |
| One meal in the plan | before shift, on shift, after shift, lunch, dinner | slot, pre-shift, post-shift | "Slot" stays in the spec |
| A meal from the fixed set | quick meal | gap meal | *guess: confirm with the team* that the fixed set is simple meals the nurse cooks or buys |
| Too old to eat | past its use-by date | expired, outside the use-by window | "Past use-by" in tags |

## Findings

### C1 · Blocker · The banner describes something that didn't happen

**Where:** Home › banner after a shift chip is tapped (S8)
**Before:** `Rota mutation detected. Regenerate plan?`
**After:** `Shifts changed. Meal plan updated.`
**Why:** the plan rebuilds itself on every change, so "Regenerate plan?" tells a nurse something still needs doing when it doesn't, and the question has no buttons to answer it. "Mutation" is a code word, and to someone who works in a hospital it isn't a neutral one.
**Rule:** true; no jargon; banners that ask need answers

### C2 · Blocker · Food past its use-by date vanishes without a word

**Where:** Meal plan (S40, missing); Home › fridge count (S10); Fridge › dish list (S28)
**Before:** *(nothing; Stew, prepped Sun 15 Nov, is left out of the plan silently)* and `10 servings stored`, which counts Stew's 2 portions
**After:**
- Meal plan, above the first day: `Stew is past its use-by date, so it's not in the plan.`
- Home: `8 portions in the fridge · 2 past use-by`
- Fridge list, on that dish: `Past use-by` in place of the prep date
**Why:** the nurse sees 10 portions on Home, 8 in the plan, and no reason for the difference. She either distrusts the plan or eats the stew because nothing told her not to. The PRD requires expired portions to be listed as past use-by (R7).
**Rule:** missing string; true; tone ladder (risk: calm, specific)

### C3 · Blocker · A blank date silently means today

**Where:** Fridge › date field (S21, S22)
**Before:** placeholder `YYYY-MM-DD`, and nothing saying that a blank field is saved as today
**After:** label `Prepped on`, hint `Leave blank for today`
**Why:** a nurse adding Sunday's batch on Tuesday leaves the date blank because it looks optional, and the dish gets two extra days before its use-by. The placeholder also vanishes as soon as she types, taking the format with it.
**Rule:** hints say what isn't obvious, including defaults; every field has a visible label

### C4 · Major · One error for three problems, in code

**Where:** Fridge › error under the fields (S23)
**Before:** `Error: invalid input (qty must be > 0, date must match YYYY-MM-DD)`
**After:** one message per problem, under its field:
- Dish empty: `Enter a dish name`
- Portions empty: `Enter how many portions`
- Portions 0 or not a number: `Portions must be a number, 1 or more`
- Date in the wrong format: `Enter the date like 2026-11-18`, or delete this message once the field is a date picker (see Out of scope)
**Why:** one message for three fields makes a tired nurse check all three. "Invalid", "qty", "> 0" and a regex describe the code, not the fix.
**Rule:** errors say what happened and what to do; one message per problem; no "invalid"

### C5 · Major · "Optimise" promises something the product doesn't do

**Where:** Home › main button (S5); Meal plan › heading (S32)
**Before:** `Optimise` and `Your Optimised Plan`
**After:** `See meal plan` and `Meal plan`
**Why:** the button only opens the plan, which is already up to date, so pressing it and seeing nothing change feels broken. "Optimise your meals" is also the language of diet apps, which Shiftplate is not.
**Rule:** true; specific button verbs; headings are nouns; one term per concept

### C6 · Major · Placeholders stand in for labels, and one is an abbreviation

**Where:** Fridge › fields (S19, S20)
**Before:** placeholders `Dish name` and `Qty`, no labels
**After:** labels `Dish` and `Portions`; no placeholders
**Why:** placeholders disappear on the first keystroke, so a nurse interrupted mid-entry comes back to two filled boxes with no names. "Qty" is form shorthand, not a word anyone says about curry.
**Rule:** every field has a visible label; no abbreviations

### C7 · Major · Shift chips are single letters with no names

**Where:** Home › 14 shift chips (S7)
**Before:** `D`, `N`, `Off`, and a screen reader announces "D, button"
**After:** visible `Day`, `Night`, `Off`; accessible name `Fri 20 Nov, day shift` (date, then shift)
**Why:** a letter is a code to learn, and "D" read aloud means nothing. Without the date in the name, a screen reader user can't tell which of 14 identical buttons they're on.
**Rule:** plain words; hidden strings count

### C8 · Major · Dates in database format and day numbers

**Where:** Meal plan › day headings (S34); Home › day cards (S14); Fridge › dish list (S28)
**Before:** `2026-11-20 · D`, `Day 1 (D)`, `prepped 2026-11-18`
**After:** `Fri 20 Nov · Day shift`; `Today · Day shift` and `Tomorrow · Day shift`; `Prepped Wed 18 Nov`
**Why:** nurses think in weekdays and shifts. "Day 1" doesn't say which day, and an ISO date takes a moment of arithmetic each time.
**Rule:** dates the way people say them; "Today" and "Tomorrow" where shorter

### C9 · Major · Seven words for food in the fridge

**Where:** Home › fridge count (S10), fridge button (S12); Fridge › heading (S17), list heading (S26), dish rows (S27)
**Before:** `{n} servings stored`, `Manage Inventory`, `Log Prep`, `Logged Items`, `{name} × {qty} meals`
**After:** `{n} portions in the fridge` (see C2), `Add food`, `Fridge`, `In the fridge`, `{name} · {qty} portions` with `1 portion` for one
**Why:** servings, meals, items, inventory, prep, qty and portion all mean the same thing here, so a nurse can't tell whether "3 meals" is three portions or three days of food. "Meals" also clashes with the meals in the plan.
**Rule:** one term per concept; plurals handled

### C10 · Major · Plan tags use internal terms

**Where:** Meal plan › tag on each meal (S36, S37)
**Before:** `portion` and `gap meal`
**After:** `From fridge` and `Quick meal` (*guess: confirm the fixed set is simple meals to cook or buy*)
**Why:** the tag's job is to say whether to shop. "From fridge" says that; "portion" doesn't. "Gap meal" is the spec's name and makes the meal sound like a failure.
**Rule:** no internal names; tags earn their place

### C11 · Minor · Eyebrow labels

**Where:** Home (S2, S6, S9, S13); Fridge (S16); Meal plan (S31)
**Before:** `Step 1 · Rota`, `Your Shifts (14 Days)`, `Fridge`, `Upcoming Meals`, `Step 2 · Inventory`, `Step 3 · Output`
**After:** step labels `(delete)`; section labels become plain headings in sentence case: `Shifts`, `Fridge`, `Next meals`
**Why:** the screens aren't a linear wizard, so "Step 2" invents an order, and "Output" is a developer's word. Small grey uppercase labels are hard to read on a phone after a night shift. The "(14 Days)" counter repeats what the 14 chips already show.
**Rule:** no eyebrow text; no decorative counters

### C12 · Minor · The screens explain themselves

**Where:** Home (S4); Fridge (S18); Meal plan (S33)
**Before:**
- `Here you can manage your rota and our algorithm will optimise your meal slots around your shifts.`
- `This screen lets you enter dishes you have prepared. Enter the name, quantity of servings and the prep date (YYYY-MM-DD).`
- `Below is the plan generated by the algorithm. Gap meals fill any slot without a portion in the use-by window.`
**After:** all three `(delete)`. The only idea worth keeping moves to a tooltip on the `Quick meal` tag: `A simple meal for when nothing in the fridge fits.`
**Why:** each paragraph describes the interface instead of helping the next tap, and together they use six of the terms the glossary retires. With labels on the fields (C3, C6) nothing is left to explain.
**Rule:** no meta explanations; tooltips only for what labels can't say

### C13 · Minor · A welcome on every visit

**Where:** Home › heading (S3)
**Before:** `Welcome to Shiftplate!`
**After:** `(delete)`; `Shifts` becomes the first heading
**Why:** Home is the screen she opens at every rota change. A greeting with an exclamation mark, every time, is noise between her and the shift chips.
**Rule:** minimum text; no welcome lines on return screens

### C14 · Minor · "Submit" and "Success!"

**Where:** Fridge › button (S24) and toast (S25)
**Before:** `Submit` and `Success!`
**After:** `Add to fridge` and `{dish} added`, for example `Chicken curry added`
**Why:** "Submit" is a form's word, not hers. "Success!" doesn't say what succeeded, and if she tapped twice she can't tell whether one dish went in or two.
**Rule:** specific button verbs; toasts name the object, past tense, no exclamation mark

### C15 · Minor · Empty states say "No data."

**Where:** Home › fridge count (S11); Fridge › list (S30); Meal plan with an empty fridge (S41, missing)
**Before:** `No data.`, `No data.`, *(nothing)*
**After:** Home: `Fridge is empty`; Fridge list: `Nothing in the fridge yet`; Meal plan: `Nothing in the fridge, so these are all quick meals.`
**Why:** "No data" reads like an error. A new user's first plan is all quick meals, and without a line saying why, the product looks like it ignored her. The PRD asks for that note (R7).
**Rule:** empty states say what's empty and how to fill it

### C16 · Minor · "Delete" for food that was eaten

**Where:** Fridge › button on each dish (S29)
**Before:** `Delete`
**After:** `Remove`, with accessible name `Remove chicken curry`
**Why:** she's taking a dish out because she ate it or binned it, not deleting a record. Five buttons all read "Delete" to a screen reader, with nothing saying which dish.
**Rule:** plain words; destructive actions name the object

### C17 · Minor · Title Case in headings and buttons

**Where:** S6, S12, S13, S17, S26, S32
**Before:** `Your Shifts (14 Days)`, `Manage Inventory`, `Upcoming Meals`, `Log Prep`, `Logged Items`, `Your Optimised Plan`
**After:** covered by C5, C9 and C11; every rewrite is in sentence case
**Why:** Title Case reads as shouting in a small app and makes ordinary words look like features.
**Rule:** sentence case

### C18 · Polish · Meal names in the plan

**Where:** Home and Meal plan › meal rows (S15, S35)
**Before:** `Pre-shift`, `On-shift`, `Post-shift`
**After:** `Before shift`, `On shift`, `After shift`
**Why:** plainer, and "on-shift" is only hyphenated before a noun.
**Rule:** plain words

### C19 · Polish · "&" and "+" in meal names

**Where:** Quick meal list (S38)
**Before:** `Chicken & rice bowl`, `Lentil soup + bread`
**After:** `Chicken and rice bowl`, `Lentil soup with bread`
**Why:** one style for names; symbols read oddly aloud.
**Rule:** plain words

### C20 · Polish · The page title says "prototype"

**Where:** browser tab (S1)
**Before:** `Shiftplate prototype`
**After:** `Shiftplate`
**Why:** the tab and the home screen bookmark show it. Pilot nurses shouldn't be told they're using something unfinished.
**Rule:** page title matches the product

## Out of scope, for ux-review

- The banner has no action and only goes away when the main button is pressed.
- The date is a text field; a date picker that defaults to today would remove C3's hint and C4's date error.
- Removing a dish has no undo and no confirmation.
- The shift chips show no weekday or date, so it's hard to tell which day is which.
- Grey text (`#aaaaaa` on an off-white background) is likely below 4.5:1 contrast, and button focus outlines are removed.
- The toast shows for 1.5 seconds at the top of the screen, away from the thumb.
- Errors appear once below the row of fields, not under each field; C4's rewrites assume per-field placement.
- Home shows 14 days of shifts but the plan covers 7.
- The use-by window is fixed at 4 days with no per-dish override; the [research run](../../../../../product/skills/deep-research/examples/shiftplate/RESEARCH.md) recommends 3 as the default.
- The PRD's after-shift check-in (eaten, binned, still in the fridge) isn't built, so there's no copy to review for it yet.
