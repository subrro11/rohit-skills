# UX review quality checklist

Score each item **pass / weak / fail** with a one-line reason. Run it before handing over.

## Set-up

1. **The user and their conditions are named** in a line or two, and the review reflects those conditions.
2. **Three to five top tasks are named**, each with a success bar where one exists, and where each came from (brief or *assumed*).
3. **What was reviewed is stated**: version, width, device or browser, and how it was opened.

## Walking the tasks

4. **Every top task was walked step by step** in the real thing, or the review says it could only read the design and why.
5. **The task walk table** has a row per step, with taps counted against the success bar.
6. **Back, undo and exit were tried** on every screen in the walk.

## Findings

7. **Every finding names a task** (or "all tasks", with a reason).
8. **Every finding names a heuristic, state or criterion** from the references.
9. **Every finding has evidence on screen**: an element, a measurement, a behaviour or a screenshot. Opinions aren't evidence.
10. **Every fix is concrete** enough to build, not a restatement of the problem.
11. **One problem per finding.** No compound findings.
12. **Severity follows the scale**: frequency, difficulty, repetition, adjusted for the user's conditions. Silent wrong results rank above loud errors.
13. **No redesign** where a fix would do; any bigger change says why the flow itself is wrong.

## States and accessibility

14. **Every state the product needs was considered**, and each is marked triggered, not triggered (why) or not applicable.
15. **Contrast claims have ratios** computed from colour values, or are marked unmeasured.
16. **Target sizes are measured** in px at the stated width.
17. **Focus visibility and input labels were checked.**

## Output

18. **The verdict comes first** and names the one thing most likely to fail the test.
19. **Copy issues are handed off** in one line each, not rewritten.
20. **What works is listed**, so fixes don't break it.
21. **The prioritised fix list** groups findings into fix before testing, fix next and later, each with a size.
22. **Findings a usability test should confirm are named.**
23. **Only guidelines that were opened are cited**, and no measurements, quotes or results were invented.
24. **No personal data** in screenshots or text.
