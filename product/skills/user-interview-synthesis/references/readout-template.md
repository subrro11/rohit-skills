## Notes on each section

- **Answer:** lead with the verdict. A reader who stops here should know what to do next and how sure to be.
- **Results against criteria:** use the targets the PRD or test plan set before the sessions. Never set or move a target after seeing the results.
- **Themes:** order by strength, then by how much they bear on the question.
- **Contradictions:** an empty table needs one line saying none were found, and a second look.
- **Can't tell you:** sample size, who wasn't in the sample, context the sessions didn't recreate, anything measured by prediction instead of behaviour. Every gap gets what would close it.
- **Observation log:** last, because it's for checking, not reading. Keep it complete.
