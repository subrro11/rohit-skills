# Synthesis quality checklist

Score each item **pass / weak / fail** with a one-line reason. Run it before handing over, and fix every fail.

## Data and coding

1. **The question and the data are named at the top**: what the round was for, how many participants, who, which method.
2. **Everything was read before coding**, or the sample coded is stated (x of y sources).
3. **Every observation has a participant ID, a location** (task, timestamp or ticket) and a type: did, said or asked.
4. **No observation mixes what happened with what it means.**
5. **Moderator help, prototype bugs and misread tasks are recorded** next to the observations they affect.

## Themes and counts

6. **Every theme is a finding, not a topic.**
7. **Every theme counts distinct participants as x of n**, with n being everyone who had the chance to show it. No "most", "many" or "users".
8. **No theme rests on one participant.** Single-person patterns are in the outlier table.
9. **Did and said are separate columns**, and no theme is graded Strong on predictions alone.
10. **Strength grades follow the rules** in `coding-and-clustering.md`, and no insight is graded higher than its weakest load-bearing theme.

## Quotes and privacy

11. **Every quotation is verbatim** from the notes or transcript. Paraphrases carry no quotation marks.
12. **No quote is used against its context** (a remark about one task presented as a remark about the product).
13. **Participants are only P-numbers.** No names, employers, workplaces, locations or details that would identify someone to a colleague.
14. **Nothing is invented**: no quotes, participants, counts or times that aren't in the source.

## Insights and opportunities

15. **Every insight has observation, why it matters, implication and strength**, and why it matters names a goal, metric or step.
16. **Implications are directions or tests, not decisions** the data can't carry.
17. **Every opportunity is a need, not a disguised solution**, and cites insights.
18. **Prioritised opportunities have at least two candidate solutions** and a riskiest assumption with a test and a fail condition.
19. **The measurement assumption was checked**: no solution quietly breaks the metric it will be judged on.

## Honesty

20. **Contradictions are shown with both sides** and a reason for the one weighted, or the readout says none were found.
21. **Every outlier is listed**, including requests the product has ruled out, with the rule that covers it.
22. **"What this data can't tell you" is filled in**, each gap with what would close it.
23. **Results against criteria use targets set before the sessions**, unchanged.

## Output

24. **The answer comes first**, with a verdict and what would change it.
25. **"Feeds next" maps findings to the PRD or the next experiment**, with IDs.
26. **Every ID cited in the readout exists in the observation log**, and every count matches the log.
