# Shiftplate v0.2 test: moderator notes

> Fictional. Shiftplate, the participants, the sessions and every number below are invented to show the skill working. Participants are already anonymised as P1–P5; no names, hospitals or wards were recorded.

**What was tested:** Shiftplate v0.2 prototype, on the participant's own phone.
**Format:** five remote sessions, 30 minutes each, one moderator, one note-taker. Think-aloud. Times are from the start of each task, read off the recording.
**Who:** five hospital nurses on rotating 12-hour shifts who already meal prep at least some weeks. None had seen Shiftplate before.
**Not after a shift:** all sessions ran on a day off or before a shift, not straight after one.

**Tasks**

| Task | Prompt read to the participant | Target |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | Enter your next 14 days of shifts | ≤ 20 s median (PRD R1) |
| T2 | Log this fridge: 4 dishes, 10 portions in total (card shown on screen) | ≤ 2 min median (PRD R2) |
| T3 | Your Thursday has just changed from a day to a night. Update your plan | < 1 min (PRD R3) |
| T4 | Imagine you've just finished a night shift. Tell the app what you ate | none set |
| T5 | Find any prep you can't use this week | none set |

Then two questions: "Which meals in your plan use your prep?" and "Would you use this at every rota change?"

---

## P1

Rotates day/night every week. Preps on Sundays, usually 3–4 dishes.

- **T1** 0:22 done. Tapped through the days quickly, no hesitation. Corrected one day (tapped Night twice, cycled to Off, tapped again).
- **T2** 1:40 done. Typed all 4 dish names, didn't use recent dishes. Sighed at dish 3. Said: "this is a bit of homework, isn't it".
- **T3** 0:41 done. Changed Thursday, tapped Re-plan straight away. Scrolled the new week and pointed at the tags: "oh, the chilli moved to Friday".
- **T4** Done. Marked each of 3 meals one at a time (5 taps). Didn't see the "All eaten" button below the list. Said: "after a real night I'd just go to bed. I wouldn't do this."
- **T5** Found the past use-by section in about 15 s. "Rice from Monday, yeah, that's gone."
- **Which meals use your prep?** Pointed to the "From your fridge" tags and named 4 of them correctly.
- **Every rota change?** "Yes. The re-plan bit is the bit I'd pay for." (Not asked about price; offered.)

## P2

Two weeks of days, then two of nights. Lives with a partner who also cooks.

- **T1** 0:31 done. Paused before starting: "can I not just take a photo of the rota?" Then tapped through.
- **T2** 2:50 done, over target. Stopped at "Portions": "is that how many tubs, or how many meals it makes?" Entered 1 for a large lasagne, went back and changed it to 4. Asked the moderator to confirm; moderator didn't.
- **T3** 0:48 done. Re-planned without hesitating. Read out two tags: "From your fridge, curry, Wednesday night."
- **T4** Done. Used the "All eaten" button (1 tap) after scrolling down to it. Said it was "fine".
- **T5** Not found. Looked through the plan, not the fridge list, and stopped at 1:30: "I'd guess the soup? But I can't see where it says." The past use-by section was on the fridge screen.
- **Which meals use your prep?** Named the tagged meals correctly.
- **Every rota change?** "Yes." Then, unprompted: "Could my partner see this? He does half the cooking, he'd need to know what's in there."

## P3

Irregular rota, changes often at short notice. Preps "when I can, maybe every other week".

- **T1** 0:25 done.
- **T2** 1:52 done. Used recent dishes for 2 of 4. Said: "honestly this is homework after work." (Checked against the recording: said about logging in general, not about this task's length.)
- **T3** Failed. Changed Thursday to Night, then went back to the plan and said "done". The plan still showed the old week; the Re-plan button sat at the top of the rota screen and was missed. Moderator moved on at 1:20. Asked afterwards, P3 thought the plan updated itself.
- **T4** Done. Marked meals one by one (4 taps). Said: "I wouldn't do this after twelve hours. I'd forget, or I'd lie."
- **T5** Found it in about 20 s.
- **Which meals use your prep?** Named the tagged meals correctly, after the moderator re-planned for them.
- **Every rota change?** "Yes, if it does the re-plan by itself."

## P4

Days mostly, nights about one week in four. Preps big batches.

- **T1** 0:28 done. Said: "I'd want to just photograph this off the board at work."
- **T2** 3:10 done, over target. Same confusion as P2: entered 2 portions for a pot of stew, then: "wait, portions of what? Servings?" Changed it twice. Said: "it's homework, this bit."
- **T3** 0:55 done. Found Re-plan after a pause.
- **T4** Done. Marked meals one by one (4 taps). Said nothing about it.
- **T5** Found it in about 25 s.
- **Which meals use your prep?** Pointed at the tags and named them correctly.
- **Every rota change?** "Maybe. Only if putting the food in was quicker."
- **Unprompted, end of session:** "Does it tell you calories? I'm trying to lose a bit."

## P5

Rotates weekly. Most experienced on nights.

- **T1** 0:19 done.
- **T2** 1:35 done. Used recent dishes for 3 of 4. No comment.
- **T3** 0:38 done. "Oh that's clever, it's moved the pasta bake."
- **T4** Done. Marked meals one by one (6 taps, corrected one). Said: "no way after a shift. If it was one tap, maybe. Like, it assumes I ate it unless I say."
- **T5** Found it in about 10 s.
- **Which meals use your prep?** Named the tagged meals correctly.
- **Every rota change?** "Yes, definitely."
