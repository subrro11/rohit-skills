# Tier chooser

Score reach and risk separately. The tier comes from the **higher** of the two.

## Reach

| Score | Who sees it |
|---|---|
| Low | Under about 100 people, all invited, or one internal team |
| Medium | A defined segment or a percentage of existing users; noticed by people who use that area |
| High | Every user, new users, or anyone outside (press, app store, partners) |

## Risk

Ask what happens to a user if this goes wrong, not what happens to the team.

| Score | Signs |
|---|---|
| Low | Cosmetic or easy to undo. Nothing lost, no money moved, no personal data touched. |
| Medium | Wastes time, breaks a habit, changes how existing data is shown, or needs a migration. Undoable with effort. |
| High | Health, safety or food safety; money; personal or sensitive data (health-adjacent, location, schedule, children); legal or regulatory exposure; anything that can't be undone for the user. |

## Tier

| Tier | When | What the plan needs |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier 3** (light) | Reach low and risk low | Internal check, a flag, a one-line note to support, a watch list of two or three signals. Review folded into the next planning meeting. |
| **Tier 2** (standard) | Either score medium, or reach low but risk high | Staged rollout with written gates, readiness checklist, consent or user comms, a tested rollback, a dated review. |
| **Tier 1** (full) | Either score high on reach, or risk high and reach medium or more | Everything in tier 2, plus partner and public comms, capacity planning, a launch-day rota, and a formal review with a decision recorded. |

Pilot, beta and GA are **stages**, not tiers. A pilot can be tier 2 (small but sensitive) and a GA can be tier 3 (wide but trivial).

## Raise the tier when

- The launch is the first time real users touch it.
- Users can't easily leave, or leaving loses their data.
- Support can't see what users see.
- A previous launch in this area went badly.

## Write it as

> **Tier 2.** Reach is low (20 invited people), but risk is high (food safety and schedule data), so the higher score sets the tier.
