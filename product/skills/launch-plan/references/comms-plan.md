## Consent message checklist

- What it is, in one sentence, and what it is not (for example, not medical or dietary advice).
- Exactly what data it keeps, and what it never collects.
- How long the data is kept, and how to have it deleted (with the time it takes).
- How to leave at any time, with no reason needed.
- How to get help, and when someone answers.
- What you'll ask of them (check-ins, a short interview) and how long it takes.

## Pause and rollback message checklist

- What happened, in plain words, without blame or jargon.
- What they should do right now (often: nothing, or don't rely on X).
- What's affected and what isn't, especially their data.
- When they'll hear next.

## Partners

List anyone outside the team who could be surprised: a venue, a platform, an advisor, an employer whose staff take part. For each, decide on purpose whether to tell them, and write down why.
