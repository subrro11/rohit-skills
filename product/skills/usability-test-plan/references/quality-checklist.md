# Test plan quality checklist

Score each item **pass / weak / fail** with a one-line reason. Run it before handing over.

## Focus

1. **The riskiest assumption is named** in one line at the top, and the reason it's the riskiest is given.
2. **Three to five research questions**, each answerable by watching the prototype, and at least one about understanding, not just completion.
3. **Every task maps to a research question**, and every research question has at least one task.
4. **Out of scope is stated**: what the prototype can't answer, and what this test won't measure.

## Participants

5. **Participants are described by behaviour**, with must-haves, exclusions, and anyone close to the idea excluded.
6. **The number is stated with a reason** that matches the method: qualitative, iterative, per user group. No claim that five people give a reliable rate.
7. **The screener hides the purpose**, uses multiple choice with believable distractors, includes one open question, and has a rule for each question.
8. **Recruitment data handling is stated**: where it lives and when it's deleted.

## Tasks

9. **No scenario names a button, tab or screen** in the product, or describes the steps.
10. **Each task asks people to act**, not to say what they'd do, and has a clear end.
11. **Each task has a starting state and a reset.**
12. **Fake data is supplied on a card**, so nobody needs to use their own life.
13. **Tasks are in a realistic order**, and the riskiest one can't be squeezed out by time.

## Criteria

14. **Every task has pass, pass with help and fail signals, and a target**, written before testing.
15. **Timings and thresholds copied from the PRD cite their requirement or gate ID**; anything new is marked *proposed*.
16. **The plan ends in a pass, iterate or stop rule** tied to the next gate.
17. **Timed tasks are run without think-aloud**, and the script says so.

## Script

18. **Session blocks add up to the session length.**
19. **Consent says what's recorded, who sees it, how long it's kept**, and that people can stop and still get the incentive. Recording starts only after a clear yes.
20. **Warm-up questions are about recent real behaviour**, not the product.
21. **Prompts are neutral**, there's a "must not say" list, and a rule for when someone is stuck.
22. **There's a comprehension check** and a debrief with open questions.

## Notes and scoring

23. **Roles are set**: moderator, note-taker, silent observers.
24. **The grid has one row per participant per task** and records outcome, time, path, hesitations, words, help and severity.
25. **Outcomes are scored on levels and reported as counts**, never averaged, with a severity scale for problems.

## Honesty

26. **No results are invented or predicted.** The plan describes what will be measured.
27. **No names or personal details** of participants appear in the plan, and the product isn't named in recruiting copy.
