# Showcase: Shiftplate research

A real research pass on a fictional product. Shiftplate is the meal-planning app for nurses on rotating 12-hour shifts from the [PRD Generator showcase](../../../prd-generator/examples/shiftplate/). Its PRD left two lines open: "Why now: Evidence needed" and a 4-day use-by default to check against food-safety guidance. This run picks those up.

[Read the report](RESEARCH.md)

Moments worth looking at:

- **The use-by default changes.** The guidance says 3–4 days, so the report recommends 3 days as the default and treats 4 as the limit.
- **Blocked pages aren't cited.** The USDA and FoodSafety.gov pages returned 403, so the report cites a university extension page that restates them, grades it Medium, and logs the primary pages under *Found, not read*.
- **It lists what it didn't do.** The contrarian question and the pressure test on the empty cell weren't run, so they're Evidence needed lines rather than silent gaps.
- **News comes back empty, and says so.** No dated competitor news was read, so the section says that and adds an Evidence needed line instead of filling the gap.
- **The positioning map has an empty cell, and the report asks why.** Nobody leads with food already prepped for shift workers. The report flags that this could mean an opening or no demand, and points to the pilot as the test.
