# Research quality checklist

Score each item **pass / weak / fail** with a one-line reason. Run it before handing over.

## Decision and plan

1. **The decision and the subject are named** at the top (company or idea), and the answer speaks to the decision.
2. **Stages ran in order**: company or problem, competitors, news, trends, frameworks. Skipped stages are skipped on purpose.
3. **No more than seven questions**, each answerable by a source and each linked to the decision.

## Sources

4. **Every cited source was opened and read** in this session. No snippet-only citations.
5. **Primary sources are used where they exist.** A secondary source is cited only when the primary wouldn't load, and the report says so.
6. **Every source has a date**: published or updated, plus the date it was read.
7. **At least one disconfirming search** was run on the claim the idea depends on most, and at least one contrarian question was asked. Both results are reported.
8. **Pages that couldn't be read are in *Found, not read***, not in the findings.

## Claims

9. **Every claim has a ledger ID**, or is marked *inference* or *user-supplied*.
10. **Every number keeps its year, sample and denominator**, in the source's own terms.
11. **Every ledger row has a quote** that contains the claim.
12. **Confidence follows the ledger rules**, and each answer's grade is no higher than its evidence supports.
13. **Disagreements are shown**, not averaged away, with a reason for the one you weight.
14. **Quotation marks are verbatim** and fit their context. Everything else is paraphrased without marks.
15. **Absence claims name the pages searched**, and no source's silence is read as an exclusion.
16. **The verification pass ran** on the load-bearing claims, and its line sits at the top of the ledger.

## Stages

17. **Competitors include price, a traction signal and the do-nothing workaround**, each is marked sourced or *inference*, and each capability is marked shipped, announced, in beta or claimed.
18. **Every news item has the date it happened**, inside the stated window, and was read, not taken from a headline. Share prices and analyst notes aren't counted as news.
19. **Every trend has two independent sources**, or is labelled a signal.
20. **Frameworks are filled with IDs or *inference***, never guesses, and their empty cells became "Evidence needed:" lines.

21. **The biggest gap was pressure-tested**: why it exists, who benefits, and whether anyone has tried to close it.

## Output

22. **The answer comes first** and ends with a recommendation and what would change it.
23. **"What the research contradicts" is filled in**, or says plainly that nothing was contradicted.
24. **Every unsettled question is an "Evidence needed:" line** with what would settle it.
25. **"Feeds the PRD" maps findings to sections**, with no empty rows.
26. **No personal data** from forums or reviews, and no search used the user's private product details.
